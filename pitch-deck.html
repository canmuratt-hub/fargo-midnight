The Problem 02 / 08

Modern Commerce Forces Toxic Data Over-Disclosure

1. B2B Trade & Net-30 Supply Chains

To qualify for supplier trade credit, enterprises must expose bank statements, total credit lines, and invoice amounts to third-party portals — risking commercial espionage and competitor leakage.

2. Regulated Retail & Identity Friction

Proving statutory age (18+/21+) or KYC compliance forces buyers to surrender raw birthdates and identity documents to dozens of merchant databases that become honeypots for breaches.

FARGO — Problem Definition Public blockchains expose amounts; Web2 silos leak PII.

Why Midnight Network 04 / 08

Midnight Dual-Ledger State Separation

Private Witness State (Kept Local)

  • local_secret_key — Actor root key
  • private_buyer_age — Exact buyer age
  • private_kyc_tier — KYC/KYB credential
  • private_credit_limit — Corporate credit line
  • private_order_amount — Confidential invoice USD

Midnight Public Ledger (Selectively Disclosed)

  • merchant_policies — Public rule thresholds
  • buyer_pseudonym — Unlinkable per-merchant ID
  • commitment — Salted order attestation hash
  • used_nullifiers — Replay & double-spend set
  • OrderStatus — Verified / Settled lifecycle
fargo.compact (Compact v0.22 / compactc 0.30.0) Zero PII · Zero Competitor Pricing Leakage
